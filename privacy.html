Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

About the application

OpenClaw Calendar is a personal integration connecting an AI assistant to the owner's Google Calendar.

Information accessed

With the user's authorization, the application may access calendar names, event details, dates, times, attendees and availability information, depending on the permissions granted.

How information is used

Calendar information is used to answer calendar-related requests and to create, modify or delete calendar events when instructed by the user.

Storage and third-party processing

The integration runs in a user-controlled environment. Authentication credentials and calendar information may be processed or stored by the configured local software and connected AI services. The exact storage, retention and service providers depend on the user's configuration.

Data sharing

Calendar information is not sold. Information may be processed by configured service providers as necessary to perform requested functions.

Security

Access to Google Calendar is controlled through Google OAuth authorization. Users can revoke access through their Google Account settings.

Data deletion

The owner can revoke access at https://myaccount.google.com/connections and remove locally stored authentication credentials and application data.
